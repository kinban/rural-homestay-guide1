# Cultural Boundaries & Environmental Protocols

A guide for hosts to set clear expectations and protect local heritage, sacred spaces, and natural resources.

---

## 1. Respecting Local Culture & Boundaries
- **Sacred Spaces:** Clearly inform guests which areas (temples, sacred groves, private community spaces) require specific etiquette or restricted entry.
- **Photography Etiquette:** Always ask guests to seek verbal permission before photographing village elders, children, or religious rituals.
- **Dress Code & Local Norms:** Share recommended clothing guidelines in advance to ensure guests feel comfortable and respect local sensibilities.

---

## 2. Waste Management & Resource Conservation
- **Plastic Policy:** Encourage a "Pack-it-in, Pack-it-out" rule where guests take non-recyclable single-use plastic back to the city.
- **Dry Toilets & Sanitation:** Provide simple, visual instructions for compost or dry toilets if municipal sewage is unavailable.
- **Resource Consciousness:** Remind guests about seasonal water scarcity or firewood usage limits during peak seasons.

---

## 3. Pre-Arrival WhatsApp Guest Brief (Template)

Copy and edit this message to send to guests before they arrive:

> *"Namaste! We are excited to welcome you to [Village Name]. To help us maintain our community's harmony, please note:*
> 1. Please carry a reusable water bottle; filtered water is provided.
> 2. Ask before taking photos of locals or sacred spaces.
> 3. Carry back any non-biodegradable waste (chips packets, plastic bottles) with you.
> 
> *Looking forward to hosting you!"*
