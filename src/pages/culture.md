# 🌿 Cultural Boundaries, Packing & Eco-Protocols

Guidelines for hosts to communicate sacred boundaries, chemical-free zone rules, terrain packing lists, and pre-arrival guest briefs.

---

## 1. Organic Space & Chemical Restrictions

* **Chemical Insect Repellents & Sunscreens:** If your village uses greywater for organic farming or relies on natural streams, request that guests avoid harsh chemical sprays.
* **Natural Alternatives:** Provide or recommend natural insect repellents (e.g., citronella spray, neem oil) and biodegradable soaps.
* **Personal Medical Kit:** Explicitly remind guests to carry their full personal supply of prescription medications, as rural pharmacies only carry basic first-aid items.

---

## 2. Terrain & Seasonal Packing List

* **Footwear Notice:** Inform guests about trail conditions (e.g., *"15-minute steep trail walk from jeep drop-off; sturdy non-slip trekking shoes required"*).
* **Monsoon Gear:** Rain ponchos, waterproof backpack covers, and leech protection socks.
* **Winter Gear:** Layered thermal wear, woolens, and warm socks (nights can be significantly colder than days).
* **Reusables:** Reusable water bottle, cloth carry bags, and a personal headlamp/flashlight.

---

## 3. Sacred Spaces & Photography Etiquette

* **Sacred Boundaries:** Inform guests in advance about restricted areas (e.g., sacred groves, specific temple inner sanctums, or private family altars).
* **Photography Consent:** Require guests to ask for verbal permission before photographing village elders, children, or private homes.
* **Dress Code:** Share clear guidelines regarding appropriate clothing inside temples and community spaces.

---

## 4. Pre-Arrival WhatsApp Brief (Template)

Copy and customize this brief to text guests 3 days before their departure:

> *"Namaste! We are excited to welcome you to [Village Name]. To ensure a comfortable trip, please note:*
> 1. **Terrain & Shoes:** Wear sturdy walking/trekking shoes for the trail to the homestay.
> 2. **Eco Rule:** We avoid chemical bug sprays to protect our water and soil. We provide natural citronella oil.
> 3. **Medical Supply:** Please bring all required personal medications for the full duration of your stay.
> 4. **Packing:** Bring a reusable water bottle, flashlight, and warm layers for the evening.
> 
> *Looking forward to hosting you!"*
