# 🗓️ Fixed-Departure Itineraries & Stay Logistics

A practical host framework for structuring 3-day weekend stays and 7-day immersion cohorts, while setting transparent expectations around terrain, food, and utilities.

---

## 1. Day-Wise Sample Itineraries

### Option A: The 3-Day Weekend Trail (Sample Structure)
* **Day 1: Arrival & Settlement**
  * *Afternoon:* Arrival, local herbal tea welcome, orientation walk to map village boundaries and private spaces.
  * *Evening:* Sunset point walk, traditional home-cooked meal, hearthside orientation.
* **Day 2: Landscape & Culture Immersion**
  * *Morning:* Guided morning nature walk (birding & endemic flora) followed by traditional breakfast.
  * *Afternoon:* Hands-on activity (kitchen garden work or local culinary session).
  * *Evening:* Hearthside dinner and local folklore storytelling.
* **Day 3: Local Pantry & Departure**
  * *Morning:* Visit local village artisans/farmers to purchase local produce directly.
  * *Midday:* Farewell lunch and coordinated return transport departure.

### Option B: The 7-Day Deep Immersion Cohort (Sample Structure)
* **Days 1–2: Orientation & Family Settlement**
  * Arrival, terrain acclimatization, understanding village ecology, and family meal sharing.
* **Days 3–4: Hands-On Artisanship & Foraging**
  * Dedicated workshops with local woodworkers, weavers, or potters; guided foraging walks.
* **Days 5–6: High Pasture Trek & Community Contribution**
  * Full-day day-hike to high pasture or neighboring hamlet; structured community activity (e.g., trail cleanup, stream maintenance).
* **Day 7: Village Pantry & Feedback Circle**
  * Purchasing local products, hosting a farewell feedback circle, and coordinating departure logistics.

---

## 2. Arrival Logistics & Transit ("First-to-Last Mile")

* **Nearest Transit Hub:** Detail the closest major railway station, bus terminal, or airport, including average travel time.
* **Vetted Local Driver Network:** Share direct phone numbers of local shared jeep or taxi drivers who know the exact homestay location.
* **Departure Coordination:** Help guests pre-book return shared transport 24 hours prior to checkout.

---

## 3. Accommodation, Washroom & Utility Audit

* **Rooms & Sleeping:** Detail room setups (e.g., wooden cots, traditional floor bedding, cotton quilts, window screens).
* **Washrooms:** Clearly disclose setup (attached vs. shared, western vs. squat toilets, running water vs. bucket hot water heated via firewood/solar).
* **Cellular Network Matrix:**
  * Provider A (e.g., Airtel): 4G for calls and light browsing.
  * Provider B (e.g., Jio): Weak / No signal.
* **Electricity Reality:** Disclose average daily power cuts and detail backup options (solar lamps, charging stations, power banks).
