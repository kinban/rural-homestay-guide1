# Zero-Cost Digital Setup for Homestay Hosts

Connect directly with city travelers without paying commission fees to middleman platforms or aggregators.

---

## 1. Setting Up Google Maps (Location Pin)
1. Open the **Google Maps** app on your mobile phone.
2. Tap **Contribute (+)** at the bottom > **Add Place**.
3. Name: `[Your Homestay Name] - Homestay`.
4. Category: Select `Homestay` or `Guest House`.
5. Location: Drag the map pin precisely to your house front door.
6. Photos: Add 3–4 clear photos of the room, clean washroom, and view.

---

## 2. WhatsApp Business Setup
1. Download the free **WhatsApp Business** app.
2. Set up your Business Profile with your Homestay Name, Address, and Available Rooms.
3. **Add Catalog Items:** Create catalog entries for "Room Nightly Rate" and "Home-cooked Meal".
4. **Greeting Message:** Enable an automated response: *"Welcome to [Homestay Name]! Reply with 1 for Room Availability, 2 for Rates, 3 for Directions."*

---

## 3. Direct Commission-Free Payments
- Display a printed **UPI QR Code** (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM) near the entrance.
- Request a 20% advance transfer via UPI to confirm bookings and purchase ingredients beforehand.
