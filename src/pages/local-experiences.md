# 🌾 Local Experiences, Foraging & Community Giving

Guidelines for hosts to structure authentic village activities, connect guests with local artisans, sell local produce directly, and enable non-intrusive community contributions.

---

## 1. Curating Local Activities & Skill Workshops

* **Culinary Workshops:** Host interactive cooking sessions focusing on seasonal, hyper-local recipes (e.g., stone-ground chutneys, traditional millet breads, hearth cooking).
* **Artisan & Craft Sessions:** Connect guests with local craftspeople (woodcarvers, weavers, potters, blacksmiths) for 2-hour hands-on sessions. *Ensure guests pay the artisan directly for their time and material.*
* **Village Fetes & Seasonal Festivals:** Maintain a shared village calendar so fixed departures coincide with local fairs, harvest celebrations, or temple rituals.

---

## 2. Guided Nature & Foraging Walks

* **Mushroom & Wild Edible Foraging:** Pair guests with knowledgeable village elders for seasonal foraging (e.g., wild greens, edible mushrooms, medicinal plants).
* **Ethical Foraging Rules:** Ensure guests follow a "take only what is needed" protocol and never forage without explicit host supervision.
* **Birding & Forest Walks:** Map out 2–3 easy-to-moderate walking trails surrounding the village for self-guided or elder-led bird watching.

---

## 3. The "Village Pantry" (Direct Local Produce Sales)

* **Setting Up a Pantry Corner:** Set aside a dedicated shelf at the homestay showcasing local produce.
* **Example Items:** Raw forest honey, dried herbs, hand-knitted woolen wear, local millets, spices, and handmade crafts.
* **Transparent Pricing:** Clearly list prices and pay 100% of sales directly to the producing family or village cooperative.

---

## 4. Structured Community Contribution (Giving Back)

* **Skill Sharing:** Allow guests to contribute in meaningful, non-intrusive ways (e.g., conducting conversational English games at the school, assisting with farm work).
* **Environmental Drives:** Organize plastic cleanup walks along popular village trails or riverbanks.
* **Community Fund:** Direct 10% of every booking toward a transparent village fund for public works like pathway repairs or street lighting.
