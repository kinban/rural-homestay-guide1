# 📋 Village Homestay Readiness Checklist

> 🎯 **For:** Village Hosts & Community Coordinators  
> ⏱️ **Read Time:** 3 minutes  

---

## 1. Safety & Cultural Boundaries

> ⚠️ **Important First Step:**
> Always gain explicit consent from local elders or village council before listing a new homestay location.

* **Private vs. Shared Spaces:** Clearly mark family living areas as private using local fabric or wooden signs.
* **Photography Rules:** Establish clear guidelines regarding photos of children, sacred groves, and village altars.

---
## 2. Accommodation, Washroom & Utility Audit (By Traveler Profile)

Different traveler groups have distinct safety, comfort, and utility needs. Hosts should audit their facilities against these profile requirements before accepting bookings.

---

### 🧕 1. Solo Female Travelers
> 🛡️ **Primary Focus:** Personal physical safety, privacy, and clear communication.

* **Door & Window Security:** Ensure guest room doors have sturdy internal latches or bolts (not just external padlocks). Ground-floor windows should have secure shutters or grilles.
* **Washroom Privacy:** Washrooms must have opaque curtains/doors, interior locks, and zero clear sightlines from communal or family spaces.
* **Host Communication:** Provide the direct phone numbers of the female head of the household and a vetted local driver or village coordinator.
* **Arrival Protocols:** Avoid late-night trail arrivals. Coordinate jeep drops so the guest is met at the main road before sunset.

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 2. Families with Children
> 🧸 **Primary Focus:** Hygiene, safety hazards, and meal flexibility.

* **Hazard Proofing:** Secure open balcony railings, steep unlit staircases, and farm tools/pesticides stored near living areas.
* **Bedding & Sleeping Setup:** Provide options for extra floor mattresses or contiguous sleeping arrangements for parents and young children.
* **Meal Customization:** Keep mild, non-spicy food options available (e.g., plain rice, boiled lentils, milk, fresh fruit).
* **Sanitation:** Ensure a dedicated closed bin for diapers and wet wipes, with clear instructions on disposal (do not flush in pit latrines).

---

### 👴 3. Older Adults & Senior Travelers
> 🦯 **Primary Focus:** Accessibility, seating support, and thermal comfort.

* **Trail & Entry Access:** Identify the number of steps and steepness of the approach trail. Have a host or local porter ready to assist with luggage.
* **Seated Washroom Setup:** Provide a sturdy chair/stool in the bathroom for bucket baths and ensure Western-style seating or a toilet chair attachment is available if needed.
* **Bed Height & Bedding:** Ensure beds are raised off the cold floor (not low floor matting) to assist individuals with knee or joint pain.
* **Warmth & Draft Protection:** Check room doors and windows for cold night drafts; provide heavy quilts and hot water bottles during winter.

---

### 💻 4. Remote Workers & Digital Nomads
> 📶 **Primary Focus:** Power stability, connectivity, and ergonomic work surfaces.

* **Cellular Data Verification:** Explicitly state network speeds (e.g., *"Airtel 4G: 15 Mbps / Jio: No Signal"*) and identify specific signal "hotspots" on the property.
* **Charging & Power Outlets:** Provide at least two working multi-plug outlets near a table or seating area.
* **Dedicated Workspace:** Offer a sturdy table and comfortable chair with adequate lighting for daytime laptop work.
* **Quiet Hours:** Define quiet windows during the day if the family or farm activities generate significant noise.

---

### 🧭 5. General / Outdoor & Adventure Travelers
> 🎒 **Primary Focus:** Gear management, drying spaces, and high-calorie sustenance.

* **Gear Storage & Drying:** Provide dedicated hooks or outdoor covered clotheslines for drying wet muddy boots, rain ponchos, or trekking gear.
* **Calorie-Dense Meals:** Offer early-morning packed breakfasts (e.g., boiled eggs, stuffed flatbreads) for sunrise trail starts.
* **Local Trail Briefings:** Keep a hand-drawn route map or contact list of certified local guides for high-altitude or forest treks.

---
