# Village Homestay Readiness Checklist

Before hosting your first guest, ensure your home and community are prepared.

## 1. Cultural & Privacy Boundaries
- Define spaces that are **private** vs. **shared**.
- Set clear community rules regarding photography and local sacred sites.

## 2. Waste & Sanitation
- Ensure clean drinking water is available.
- Set up segregated bins for organic waste and inorganic trash.
